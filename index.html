🎬 Curriculum Snapshot

5 Modules, Project by Project

The full Premiere Pro syllabus

Click a module to see every class and its end-of-module assignment.

Camera types, controls & the exposure triangle. Starting a project, workspace tour, importing & organizing media. Sequences, the source window & timeline workflow. Effect Controls — scale, position, opacity, trimming. Match cut effect, nesting, rate stretch, basic export. Assignment: Match Cut.

Razor cuts, ripple edits, slips & advanced trim tools. Masking & mask transitions. Track matte key & luma mattes (compositing). Effects panel, transitions & importing presets. Zoom keyframe effects & preset saving. Assignment: Movie Trailer project.

Keyframing with the pen tool, audio keyframes. Speed & duration, speed ramping. Speed ramping curves. Cinematic overlays, split-screen & stabilization. Green screen removal (Ultra Key/Luma Key), blend modes. Assignment: Speed Ramping project.

Audio-video sync & talking-head editing. B-roll & multi-camera editing. Types of cuts — theory (straight, jump, cutaway, match cut & more). Types of cuts in practice — J-cuts & L-cuts. Titles & graphics with Essential Graphics, importing Mogrts. Assignment: Interview Editing project.

Music video timeline arrangement. Multi-camera cuts, B-rolls & effects between cuts. Understanding log footage. Color correction with Lumetri — white balance, exposure, saturation. Color grading with Lumetri Creative. Audio editing & SFX. Assignment: Music Video Edit project.

Motion graphics & 2D animation modules will be added here. Stay tuned for the After Effects track launch.

🎬 After Effects Track

After Effects — Motion Graphics & VFX

Full After Effects syllabus — 5 Modules + Compositing

Click a module to see every class and its end-of-module assignment.

AE interface, workspace, composition, layers & timeline. Transform properties — position, scale, rotation, opacity, anchor point. Keyframes & keyframe interpolation — easy ease, speed graph & value graph. 12 Principles of Animation — squash & stretch, anticipation, staging, follow through & overlapping action. Parenting, null objects, pick whip & pre-compositions. Rigid-body motion design — overshoot, bounce, follow through & secondary animation. Assignment: Bouncing Ball + Car Animation.

Shape layers — rectangle, ellipse, polygon, star, fill & stroke. Shape layer animation — trim paths, repeater, merge paths & offset paths. Parenting shapes & creating complex motion from simple objects. Shape morphing — transforming one shape into another. Loops & expressions — loopOut(), simple repeating animations & controls. Assignment: Butterfly Animation.

Text layers — character, paragraph, alignment, tracking, leading & text controls. Text animators — position, scale, rotation, opacity & range selector. Text reveals — masks, mattes, wipes & character-by-character animation. Kinetic typography — timing text to music, rhythm & visual hierarchy. Advanced typography — text transitions, overshoot, motion blur & secondary animation. Typography workflow — combining text, shapes, effects, transitions & sound. Assignment: Text Animation + Lower Third Animation.

Animated icons, illustrations & graphic elements. Graphic/logo prep — Illustrator/Photoshop assets, vector layers & asset organization. Reveals — masks, track mattes, luma mattes & animated reveals. Glow, CC Light Sweep, blur & light-based effects. Fractal noise, turbulent noise — procedural backgrounds & textures. Turbulent displace, wave warp & displacement map — distortion techniques. Motion tile & CC Repetile — seamless backgrounds & infinite motion. Gradient ramp, fill, tint & stylization. Gaussian, directional & radial blur — speed, zoom & transition effects. Expressions deep dive — wiggle(), time, valueAtTime, linking properties & pick-whip. Sound design basics for motion graphics — syncing whooshes, impacts & foley to animation. Assignment: Motion Poster.

3D layers, cameras & lights — basics of 3D space in After Effects. Building a 2.5D parallax environment from flat 2D artwork (layer splitting & depth placement). Camera rigging with null objects, depth of field & focus pulls. Camera movement techniques — dolly, pan, truck & orbit. Native 3D extrusion — extruding text & shapes with the Cinema 4D renderer. Materials, lighting & shadows in 3D space. Compositing 3D elements with 2D footage (product/commercial style). Rendering & delivery — Render Queue vs. Media Encoder, codecs & alpha channels. Building Motion Graphics Templates (MOGRTs) for Premiere Pro's Essential Graphics panel. Assignment: Camera Animation (Depth & 2D Environment) + 3D Commercial — Final Project.

Classes 1–5: Intro to VFX compositing — layer blending modes, track mattes & alpha channels. Manual masking — pen tool, bezier masks, mask modes & feathering. Roto Brush 2 & Refine Edge — fast subject isolation. 2D point tracking — single point, multi-point & perspective corner pin. Attaching & blending graphics/screens onto tracked footage. Warp Stabilizer. Assignment: Mask Transition + TV Screen / Hoarding Replacement.

Classes 6–10: 3D camera tracker workflow — solving camera motion from 2D footage. Placing 3D text, solids & CG elements into tracked scenes. Color correction fundamentals — Lumetri Color, curves & color balance. Color grading & cinematic look development. Speed ramping & speed curves. Assignment: Speed Ramping with Color Grade & Elements.

Classes 11–16: Chroma keying — Keylight, screen matte controls & core matte cleanup. Advanced keying — spill suppression, edge refinement & choking. Color matching & integration — garbage mattes, shadows & reflections. Displacement map, fractal noise & compositing practical stock elements. Basic CG passes overview. Matching lighting, shadows & perspective for CG-to-live-action integration. Assignment: Green Screen Scene Build Up + Basic CG Integration.
